## What goes in it

- Owner dashboard: The numbers, the jobs and what needs attention, on one screen.
- Quoting engine: Your rates and margins built in, so every quote is priced the same way.
- CRM: Customers, leads and history in one place, instead of the CRM you rent and resent.
- Inventory and ordering: What you have, what is running low, and what is on order.
- Crew on their phones: Photos, receipts, hours and safety forms from the job site.
- AI doing the admin: Prices quotes, files receipts and writes the morning report. A person approves anything that moves money.

Rented software makes you work its way, charges every month, and still needs three other apps beside it. A custom app works your way, and you own it.

No. Evolve is one app we built, for Evolve Eco Blasting. Your app is built for your business. Evolve just proves the approach works on a real crew.

No. We move what matters into the new app before anything gets cancelled.

### How is AI used in the app?

It does the admin between the jobs: pricing quotes, filing receipts and writing the morning report. A person approves anything that moves money.

Questions

Frequently asked

What is Evolve?

Evolve is a business management system for service companies: quoting, dispatch, safety, receipts and invoicing on one workbook, with an AI doing the admin and a person approving every money step.

Is it only for blasting companies?

No. It was built for Evolve Eco Blasting, but nothing in it is about blasting. Trade packs set it up for other service businesses in one step.

Does it handle payments?

No. Evolve never moves money. When a customer pays by e-transfer, cheque, card or cash, a person marks the invoice paid.

Do we have to change our tools?

No. Evolve runs on a Google Sheet and the drive you already have, plus a phone app for the crew.

What does it cost to set up for my business?

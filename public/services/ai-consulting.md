## Bring your business into the AI age. Gently.

Everyone says you need AI. Fewer people can tell you what for. We look at how your business actually runs, then tell you what can be automated, what should stay human, and which old apps you can finally stop paying for. Straight answers, including "you do not need us for this one."

We nerd out on the tools so you do not have to.

## Prices


Consulting starts with the AI Ops Audit ($495). Ongoing advice is quoted to the scope, or rolled into Fractional CTO.

## What we look at

- What can be automated: The repeated, rule-based jobs that eat your evenings: quotes, receipts, follow-ups, reminders and reports.
- What should stay human: The calls, the judgment, the handshake, and anything that moves money. AI preps it. A person decides.
- Old apps worth retiring: The subscriptions you pay for, half use and quietly resent. Sometimes building your own is the cheaper move.
- Where your data lives: Who can see what, what should never go into an AI tool, and how Alberta PIPA fits in.
- What your people need to learn: The gap between "we bought it" and "we use it" is usually training, not software.

## How it goes

- Listen first: we learn how the business runs before anyone says the word "agent."
- A written plan: what to do, in what order, what it costs, and what to skip.
- No dependency games: we set it up, train your people to run it, and stay on only if you want us to.
- Honest scope: if a twenty dollar app already does the job, we will say so.

## Frequently asked

### What does an AI consultant actually do for a small business?

Finds the work AI can take off your plate, tells you what it costs and what to leave alone, and then helps you build it or hands you a plan to build it yourself.

### Where do we start?

With the AI Ops Audit: one week, $495, and a written plan you keep whoever builds it.

### Will you tell us to buy a pile of new software?

Usually the opposite. Most of the time we connect what you have, retire what you do not need, and build the missing piece.

### Do you consult outside Edmonton?

Yes. We are based in Edmonton, work in person across Alberta, and work remotely with businesses anywhere in Canada.

### What does AI consulting cost?

The AI Ops Audit is $495. Ongoing consulting is quoted to the scope. Fractional CTO is $5,000 a month for the build months.

## Frequently asked

### What is a company brain?

One organized home for how your business works: processes, prices, brand voice, templates and documents, structured so both your team and your AI tools can use it.

### How is it different from a shared drive?

A drive stores files. A company brain organizes the knowledge in them by department and decision, keeps it current, and controls who can change what.

### Will it work with the AI tools we already use?

Yes. The point is to give the AI you already use a real baseline to work from, so it answers from your facts instead of guessing.

### Is it only for big companies?

No. It works for one person or a thousand. Small teams feel it first, because so much lives in one or two heads.

Questions

Frequently asked

What is a fractional CTO?

A senior technology lead you share part-time. You get the decisions, the builds and the accountability without hiring for it full time.

Who is it for?

Small and mid-sized businesses that have outgrown duct-taped tools but do not need a full-time technology executive.

What does it cost?

Two build months at $5,000 a month for up to 80 hours each, then $3,950 a month for up to 60 hours.

Can we stop?

Yes. After the two-month minimum, give 30 days' notice.

How is it different from a Full Business Overhaul?

An overhaul is a fixed-price project. Fractional CTO is an ongoing monthly role. Plenty of companies do one, then the other.

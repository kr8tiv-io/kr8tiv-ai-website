Your business, in a form software can act on.

The problem it solves

Most AI fails at work for a boring reason.

Ask a general-purpose assistant to quote a job and it will write you something beautiful and wrong. It doesn't know your rates, your margins, which customer this is, what you charged them last time, or that the deposit already cleared. It has language, not context.

JARVIS is the missing half. It keeps one current, structured picture of the operation and exposes it to every agent that needs it. The quoting agent prices against your real rate table. The dispatch agent knows what is already booked. The digest agent reads the same numbers your accountant will.

What it does

Four jobs, running whether or not anyone is watching.

  • 01

    Memory

    One source of truth per business, kept current by the agents that touch it. No second system to reconcile, and no answer that depends on which document someone opened.

  • 02

    Watchers

    Standing conditions evaluated continuously — deposit in but unscheduled, invoice unpaid past terms, quote expiring, job finished but not billed. Each raises a specific action with a name and a date on it.

  • 03

    Scheduled runs

    Reports and reconciliations that fire on a clock instead of on a reminder: the morning digest, the weekly review, the month-end tidy-up.

  • 04

    Audit trail

    Every write is attributable and reversible. When an agent changes something, you can see what, when, and on what basis — which is the difference between automation you trust and automation you babysit.

How it's deployed

It runs in your accounts, on your stack.

  • No migration. JARVIS reads and writes through MCP-native connectors into the spreadsheets, drive, and mail you already use.
  • No new seats to buy. Your team keeps working in the software they already know; the engine sits underneath it.
  • Scoped access. Agents get only the connectors a job requires, and credentials stay in your accounts.
  • Handed over. The configuration and the source of every agent we write for you is yours at the end of the build.
  • Portable. If you change accounting software or drives, the connector changes — not the operation.

JARVIS isn't a product you log into. It is the reason the automations in your business give the right answer at 6:30 in the morning.

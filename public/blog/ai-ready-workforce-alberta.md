## Why does output per employee matter more than headcount?

Canada's scoreboard is labour productivity: real GDP per hour worked in the business sector. In the second quarter of 2026 it rose 1.0 percent, the largest quarterly increase since the second quarter of 2020. Hours worked edged down 0.1 percent. Unit labour costs still rose 0.6 percent, a fifth straight quarter. ([Statistics Canada, The Daily, 3 September 2026](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260903/dq260903c-eng.htm))

When Erik Brynjolfsson, Danielle Li and Lindsey Raymond gave a generative assistant to 5,172 customer-support agents, issues resolved per hour rose 15 percent on average. Less experienced and lower-skilled workers improved speed and quality. The most experienced saw small speed gains and small quality declines. Quarterly Journal of Economics, May 2025. ([Brynjolfsson, Li and Raymond](https://academic.oup.com/qje/article/140/2/889/7990658))

> The biggest documented gains did not come from removing the newest person. They came from giving that person the habits of the best person, with a human still on the call.

The Edmonton version is in [what AI can and cannot do](https://kr8tiv.ai/what-ai-can-and-cant-do-for-your-business/). The tool spread know-how. It did not delete the job.

BCG's 2025 survey covered more than 10,600 people in 11 countries. More than three-quarters of leaders and managers use generative AI several times a week. Front-line regular use had stalled at 51 percent, and it is sharply higher after at least five hours of training plus in-person training and coaching. Only one-third said they had been properly trained. ([BCG, 2025](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2025/ai-at-work-momentum-builds-but-gaps-remain)) Five hours is a floor.

## How does the Canada-Alberta Productivity Grant treat AI training?

On 8 October 2026 every trainee became eligible for 75 percent, employed family members could be trainees, and owners could be trainees. ([alberta.ca](https://www.alberta.ca/canada-alberta-productivity-grant), [guidelines](https://open.alberta.ca/dataset/14776419-ee5f-49d3-bfb6-9177d0214ef5/resource/cb980216-d2d1-4ea4-b4c2-df6f71c610a4/download/jeti-canada-alberta-productivity-grant-applicant-guidelines-2026.pdf))

The employer cap is $100,000 per fiscal year, 1 April to 31 March. The person cap is 75 percent, up to $5,000. More than one application is allowed. The caps still bind. You pay 100 percent first. The ministry of Jobs, Economy, Trade and Immigration decides, only through the portal, not by phone or email.

The guidelines' own example, not our price: $3,000 of eligible costs pays $2,250 from the grant and $750 from the employer. At $12,000 the grant stops at $5,000, and the employer pays $7,000.

### A $2,000 seminar, per trainee

KR8TIV's seminar is $2,000 CAD for the group, not per person. Checked on the live training page on 27 November 2026. Say eight named trainees, and the ministry accepts an even split.

- Eligible cost: $2,000 divided by 8, which is $250.
- Grant at 75 percent: $187.50 per trainee.
- Employer share: $62.50 per trainee.
- If all eight are approved: $1,500 back.
- You still write a cheque for $2,000 first.

The $5,000 cap is idle at $187.50. At $1,500 back per person, 66 people use $99,000 of the employer cap. At $5,000 each, 20 people use the whole cap. If the ministry treats the full $2,000 as one person's course, the grant is $1,500 and the employer pays $500. File the invoice you can defend. This is arithmetic, not an approval.

Training must be instructor-led, tied to the job, and done within 52 weeks of receipt. Online counts as Alberta. The provider is a third party whose main work has been training for two years, with a qualified instructor. Not you, not your staff, not a vendor teaching only its own product. Wages, licences, subscriptions, conferences, consulting, coaching and room rental are out. Trainees live in Alberta and are citizens, permanent residents or protected persons: employees, people already offered a job, employed family, or active owners. Contractors, volunteers, board members and unemployed family are out, unless the person is an owner.

Apply at [capg.alberta.ca](https://capg.alberta.ca/) at least 30 days before the start. Training that begins before the status reads Application Received is ineligible. That status flips when the trainee clicks "I confirm," not when you click submit. First come, first served. Start after receipt and before approval, and a decline means the bill is yours. Canada-Alberta Productivity Grant AI training appears as example courses: AI for Business, Generative AI Applications, Microsoft Copilot. A topic is not a purchase order. Checked 27 November 2026.

## What does a 90-day build look like?

File before you train. A course that starts before the application is received is a course you pay for alone.

**Days 1 to 15.** Name three tasks and the people, owner included if the owner does the work. Read the grant page before you pick a date.

**Days 15 to 30.** In Alberta, submit and get every trainee to click consent, so the status is Application Received, with 30 days of runway. In B.C., leave 60 days. In Manitoba, stay in the fiscal year. In Saskatchewan, use the programs that exist.

**Days 30 to 45.** One page: allowed tools, what never gets pasted in, who reviews anything that leaves the building. Start the company brain with prices and the customer-email voice.

**Days 45 to 75.** Train on the real work, out loud, skeptic in the room. Five hours is the floor.

**Days 75 to 90.** Review the outputs, count the hours, file the completion form with proof of payment.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is AI training eligible for the Canada-Alberta Productivity Grant?

It can be, if it is instructor-led, job-relevant, and from a third party that has trained for at least two years. Example topics include AI for Business and Generative AI Applications. The ministry decides. KR8TIV training is not pre-approved. Checked 27 November 2026.

### Do I have to apply before training starts?

Yes for this grant. Training that starts before Application Received is ineligible. That status arrives when the trainee clicks I confirm. Apply at least 30 days ahead. A decline after you have started means the bill is yours. Checked 27 November 2026.

### Can business owners be trainees under the Canada-Alberta Productivity Grant?

Yes, since 8 October 2026, if the owner has an active role. Employed family members can be trainees. Unemployed family, contractors and volunteers cannot, unless the person is an owner. Checked 27 November 2026.

### Will AI replace my staff?

It should not. Of 1,163 senior leaders in an Orgvue survey published 29 April 2025, 39 percent made people redundant because of AI, and 55 percent of that group called the decision wrong. The research gains are output gains for people who stay, especially less experienced staff, with a human still reviewing.

### What is a company brain?

One home for processes, prices, voice and templates, so people and tools read the same facts. KR8TIV builds one for $1,000 CAD. You own it. Checked 27 November 2026.

### What is the difference between a training grant and PrairiesCan or BDC money?

A training grant pays back part of a course. PrairiesCan is a repayable project contribution starting at $250,000. BDC LIFT is a loan you repay, from $25,000, for profitable firms at $1 million in revenue. Checked 27 November 2026.

### Does rural Alberta use AI at the same rate as the cities?

No. Urban Alberta was 19.3 percent and rural Alberta 14.4 percent in the second quarter of 2026, both quality B. Canada rural was 9.9 percent, quality A.

### How do Edmonton employers start AI training for employees?

Name the tasks, write what never goes into a tool, and file the grant before the first session. KR8TIV trains in Edmonton and across Canada, online or in person. A free one-hour demo, a micro seminar for your team, is how you see the work before you buy a day.

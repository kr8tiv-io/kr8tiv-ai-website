## Claims dropped or softened

- MIT NANDA "95% of generative AI pilots fail" figure: dropped. Widely reported but the methodology is disputed; not needed for the argument.
- HBS / BCG "more than 40% higher quality": dropped the number (working-paper figure). The article uses the published abstract's "significantly better quality".
- HBS / BCG "19 percentage points": the article uses the published version's "19% less likely".
- "AI writes the first 80%": cut, it was an invented number.
- "Has read most of the internet": replaced with "trained on a mountain of text".
- Klarna CEO quote "AI can already do all of the jobs that we, as humans, do": not used.
- Exact digest time (6:30 AM vs 6:00 AM): left out, see above.
- Opinion, labelled as such in the text: "the companies that treat AI as a reason to fire people will be the ones that struggle" is framed as KR8TIV AI's bet, backed by items 7 to 12.
